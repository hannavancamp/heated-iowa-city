Yoga Sculpt in Iowa City

Yoga sculpt takes a heated yoga flow and adds light hand weights, strength moves, and short bursts of cardio, all set to upbeat music. If a regular yoga class feels too slow for you, this is probably the one you are looking for.

What a yoga sculpt class involves

A typical class runs 60 minutes in a room heated to around 95°F. It opens with a short warm-up flow, then moves through rounds of yoga poses mixed with squats, lunges, curls, and shoulder presses using weights between 2 and 5 pounds. Most classes include a few cardio intervals, such as jumping jacks or squat jumps, before ending with a cool-down stretch.

It is more of a workout than a traditional yoga class, so expect to keep moving for most of the hour.

Both studios price sculpt the same as any other class, so the drop-in rate and monthly memberships cover it, and so do the intro offers. Prairie Flame's two-week intro for $30 is a good fit if sculpt is your main interest, since the studio offers it three times a week. Tips for your first sculpt class

  • Pick the lightest weights in the room the first time, since they feel much heavier after 40 minutes in the heat.
  • Skip the jumps if you need to. Teachers always offer a low-impact version, like stepping instead of jumping.
  • Bring a towel and more water than you think you need.
  • Set up near the back so you have room to move and can watch others when an exercise is new to you.

Is yoga sculpt good for beginners?

It can be, but many people find it easier to take two or three regular heated classes first so they know the basic poses and how their body handles the heat. If you have never tried hot yoga at all, start with our beginner's guide.
