Free Hot Yoga in Iowa City

A monthly membership is a lot to spend on something you have never tried. The good news is that every studio in the area wants new students, so there are several ways to take your first few classes for free or close to it.

Where to take a free hot yoga class
Riverbend Heat Studio

Your first class is free. Book any Warm Flow Intro class online and the studio covers it. It is the easiest way to find out whether you like heated yoga before spending anything.

Ember Hot Yoga

One week unlimited for $25. If you go four or five times that week, each class costs about $5, less than a quarter of the drop-in price.

Prairie Flame Yoga

Two weeks unlimited for $30. It is the longest intro offer in the area and gives you enough classes to try power flow, sculpt, and hot pilates before you decide.

Community and donation classes

Community classes cost less, are often taught by newer teachers, and tend to be the friendliest rooms for beginners.

Ember Hot Yoga, Sunday Community Flow. Sundays at 4 pm, pay what you can from $0 to $10.
Riverbend Heat Studio, Free First Friday. A free warm flow class on the first Friday of every month at 6 pm. Spots fill quickly, so reserve one early in the week.
Prairie Flame Yoga, Summer Park Series. Free outdoor classes in North Liberty on Saturday mornings from June through August. They are not heated, but they are a relaxed way to meet the teachers.
Student discounts

Ember Hot Yoga takes $30 off its monthly unlimited membership when you show a University of Iowa student ID, bringing it to $99. The other two studios do not advertise a student rate, but both sell class packs that work out cheaper than paying for single drop-ins.

How to stretch your budget the furthest

Use the intro offers one after another instead of all at once. Start with the free class at Riverbend Heat Studio, and if you enjoy it, move on to Ember's $25 intro week and then Prairie Flame's two-week intro. That gives you about three and a half weeks of classes at three different studios for $55, which is plenty of time to know which one you would actually pay for.

A few other habits save money as you go. Bring your own mat to skip the rental fee, which is usually around $3. Check each studio's cancellation policy, since a late cancel can count as a used class. And before you sign up for any intro offer, see whether it renews automatically into a full membership so you can cancel in time if you are not ready.
